Process

From first call to production in weeks

  1. 1

    Discover Weeks 1-2

    Map workflows, data and constraints. Score use-cases by value, feasibility and risk, and pick the first one to prove.

  2. 2

    Design Weeks 2-3

    Architecture, agent and data design, success metrics, guardrails and a clear build plan with milestones.

  3. 3

    Prove Weeks 3-6

    Working prototype on your real data, evaluated against agreed KPIs. Go / no-go with a business case.

  4. 4

    Implement Weeks 6-14

    Production build with integrations, security, evaluation harnesses and change management.

  5. 5

    Run Ongoing

    Managed operations on clock time: monitoring, tuning, cost control and monthly business reviews.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can we see results?

Most clients have a working proof of value in 4-6 weeks, built on their own data and measured against agreed KPIs.

Do you work with our existing cloud and tools?

Yes. We build on AWS, Azure or GCP and integrate with your existing CRM, ERP, ticketing and data stack. We stay model-agnostic to avoid lock-in.

How do you keep AI agents safe?

Scoped credentials, approval gates, sandboxed tool access, evaluation suites and complete audit logs - designed in from day one.

Who owns the IP?

You do. Code, prompts, data pipelines and evaluation assets built for you are yours.

What engagement models do you offer?

Fixed-scope proof of value, time-and-materials delivery, dedicated AI pods, and managed run services with SLAs.

Ready to put AI on the clock?

Tell us the workflow that costs you the most time. We'll show you what an agent can do with it.
