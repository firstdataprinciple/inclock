FAQ

How quickly can we see results?

Most clients have a working proof of value in 4-6 weeks, built on their own data and measured against agreed KPIs.

Do you work with our existing cloud and tools?

Yes. We build on AWS, Azure or GCP and integrate with your existing CRM, ERP, ticketing and data stack. We stay model-agnostic to avoid lock-in.

How do you keep AI agents safe?

Scoped credentials, approval gates, sandboxed tool access, evaluation suites and complete audit logs - designed in from day one.

Who owns the IP?

You do. Code, prompts, data pipelines and evaluation assets built for you are yours.

What engagement models do you offer?

Fixed-scope proof of value, time-and-materials delivery, dedicated AI pods, and managed run services with SLAs.
